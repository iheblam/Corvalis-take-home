# Purchasing

## Small purchases
Purchases under 250 euros can be paid directly with the company card. Keep the invoice and upload it
within one week.

## Quotes
For any purchase above 2,500 euros, ask for at least two quotes and attach them to the purchase request.

## Approved suppliers
Laptops, phones and screens are ordered only through the IT desk, from the approved supplier list.

## Subscriptions
Software subscriptions need the approval of the IT desk before the first payment, even when the cost
is low.
