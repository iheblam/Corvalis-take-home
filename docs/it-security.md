# IT security

## Passwords
Passwords must contain at least 14 characters. Use the password manager provided by the company
and never reuse a password from a personal account.

## Lost or stolen devices
Report any lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT desk within one hour, by phone or by opening
a ticket. The device will be locked and erased remotely.

## Software installation
Only software from the company catalogue may be installed. Ask the IT desk for anything else.

## VPN
The VPN must be switched on when working from a public network, such as a hotel or a station.
