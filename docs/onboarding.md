# Onboarding

## First day
New employees are welcomed at 9 o'clock by their manager, who presents the team and the premises.

## Accounts
The IT desk creates the e-mail account, the badge and the access to the shared drive before the
first day. The laptop is handed over in the morning.

## Buddy
Each newcomer is paired with a buddy from another team for the first three months.

## Probation review
A review meeting takes place after three months, with the manager and HR, to discuss the first
objectives and the feedback of the team.
