# Leave and working time

## Annual leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid holiday per calendar year.
Unused days may be carried over until 31 March of the following year.

## Sick leave
Inform your manager before 9 o'clock on the first day of illness.
A medical certificate is required from the second day.

## Public holidays
The office is closed on the ten Belgian public holidays.
A public holiday falling on a Sunday is replaced by another day chosen by the company.

## Remote work
Employees may work from home up to two days per week, agreed with their manager.
Tuesday and Thursday are team days at the office.
