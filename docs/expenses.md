# Expense policy

## Scope
This policy applies to every employee and contractor who spends money on behalf of Corvalis.
Personal costs are never covered.

## Meals
When working away from the office for a full day, the evening meal is reimbursed up to 35 euros.
Lunch is covered only when a client meeting takes place outside the usual workplace.
Alcohol is never reimbursed.

## Travel
Public transport fares for business journeys are reimbursed on presentation of the receipt.
First class is accepted for journeys longer than three hours.
Taxis are allowed only late at night or when public transport is unavailable.

## Mileage
Business kilometres driven with a private car are paid at the official rate of the current year.
Commuting between home and the office is excluded.

## Approval
Every claim is approved by the direct manager. A claim above 500 euros also needs the approval
of the finance director. Claims must be submitted within 60 days.
